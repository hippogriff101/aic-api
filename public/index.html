An Inspector Calls is a play written by J. B Priestley, first performed in May 1945.

The play follows Birling family as they learn about a lower-class woman called Eva Smith who has committed suicide.

The play serves as a reminder of social responsibility in the post-war Britain of 1945 but is still studied by millions of students across the UK.
